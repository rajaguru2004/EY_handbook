# Shortcuts & Speed Tables

### EY Placement Aptitude Handbook · The Memorisation Layer

> **This document contains nothing you need to understand — only things you need to *recall instantly*.**
>
> The difference between a candidate who scores 8/12 in the numerical section and one who scores 11/12 is rarely mathematical ability. It is that the second candidate sees "37.5% of 96" and thinks *"three-eighths of 96 is 36"* while the first reaches for long multiplication. At 45 seconds per question, recall **is** the skill.
>
> **How to use this:** read it aloud once a day for a week before the test. Cover the right-hand column and test yourself. Anything you hesitate on, write out ten times.

```

   Slow:  96 × 0.375 = long multiplication          ~40 seconds

   Slow:  660/1200 × 100 = long division            ~30 seconds
   Fast:  660/1200 = 11/20 = 55%                    ~4 seconds
```

```
    n5² = n(n+1) followed by 25

    25² = 2×3 = 6, then 25   →  625
    35² = 3×4 = 12, then 25  →  1225
    45² = 4×5 = 20, then 25  →  2025

```
    (100 − a)² = (100 − 2a) followed by a²

    96² :  100 − 8 = 92,  and 4² = 16   →  9216
    93² :  100 − 14 = 86, and 7² = 49   →  8649

## 3. Multiplication Shortcuts

**Multiplying by 5, 25, 50, 125**

```
× 5    =  × 10 ÷ 2            48 × 5   = 480/2   = 240
× 25   =  × 100 ÷ 4           36 × 25  = 3600/4  = 900
× 50   =  × 100 ÷ 2           84 × 50  = 8400/2  = 4200
× 125  =  × 1000 ÷ 8          32 × 125 = 32000/8 = 4000
```

**Multiplying by 9, 99, 11**

```
× 9    =  × 10 − itself       47 × 9  = 470 − 47 = 423
× 99   =  × 100 − itself      37 × 99 = 3700 − 37 = 3663
× 11   =  add adjacent digits outward
             43 × 11 → 4 | 4+3 | 3 → 473
             68 × 11 → 6 | 6+8=14 | 8 → 7 | 4 | 8 → 748  (carry the 1)
```

**Difference of squares**

$$a^2 - b^2 = (a+b)(a-b)$$

```
   64² − 36² = (100)(28) = 2,800
   24.5² − 21² = (45.5)(3.5) = 159.25
```

**Numbers near a base**

```
   102 × 108 :  base 100, deviations +2 and +8
                (102 + 8) | (2 × 8)  →  110 | 16  →  11,016

    97 ×  96 :  base 100, deviations −3 and −4
                (97 − 4) | (3 × 4)   →  93 | 12   →  9,312
```

## 13. Mental Arithmetic Techniques

**Percentage decomposition — break into 10%, 5%, 1%**

```
35% of 640:
    10% = 64  →  30% = 192
     5% = 32
    ─────────────────────────
    35% = 224

17% of 500:
    10% = 50 · 5% = 25 · 2% = 10
    ─────────────────────────
    17% = 85
```

**The commutation trick**

$$a\% \text{ of } b = b\% \text{ of } a$$

```
16% of 25  →  25% of 16 = 4
48% of 50  →  50% of 48 = 24
84% of 25  →  25% of 84 = 21
 4% of 175 →  175% of 4 = 7
```

**Deviation method for averages**

```
Average of 246, 251, 249, 254, 250
    Assume 250.  Deviations: −4, +1, −1, +4, 0.  Sum = 0.
    Average = 250 + 0/5 = 250

Average of 82, 87, 91, 78, 92
    Assume 85.  Deviations: −3, +2, +6, −7, +7.  Sum = +5.
    Average = 85 + 5/5 = 86
```

**Cross-multiplication to compare fractions**

$$\frac{a}{b} \text{ vs } \frac{c}{d} \quad\longrightarrow\quad ad \text{ vs } bc$$

```
Which rejection rate is higher: 80/2000 or 45/1500?
    80 × 1500 = 120,000    vs    45 × 2000 = 90,000
    The first is larger  ⇒  80/2000 is the higher rate
```

**Divisibility shortcuts in DI**

```
Dividing by 400 and ×100  →  just divide by 4       (288/400 → 72%)
Dividing by 250 and ×100  →  divide by 2.5, i.e. ×0.4
Dividing by 1200          →  divide by 12, then by 100
```

## 14. The Approximation Discipline

**When to approximate**

```
✅ Options are 5% or more apart          →  round to 2 significant figures
✅ The question says "approximately"      →  approximate freely
✅ You need a magnitude check first       →  always approximate

❌ Two options are within 2% of each other →  the setter wants exact work
❌ The answer is a ratio in lowest terms   →  exact
❌ Money to the rupee                      →  exact
```

**Worked approximations**

```
"1,847 is what percent of 8,932?"
    ≈ 1,850/8,900 ≈ 1,800/9,000 = 20%
    (Exact: 20.68% — close enough to separate options at 18%, 21%, 24%)

"₹4,738 crore grew to ₹5,912 crore. Percentage growth?"
    ≈ 1,170/4,740 ≈ 1,200/4,800 = 25%
    (Exact: 24.78%)

"What is 23.7% of 5,840?"
    ≈ 24% of 5,800 ≈ 0.25 × 5,800 − 0.01 × 5,800 = 1,450 − 58 = 1,392
    (Exact: 1,384)
```

**Magnitude checks before calculating**

```
"An item costing ₹1,860 is discounted 35%. Find the selling price."
    You pay 65% ≈ two-thirds of 1,860 ≈ 1,240
    Any option far from 1,240 is wrong — including ₹651, which is the
    DISCOUNT amount rather than the selling price.

"A 20% price rise with 10% lower consumption. Change in expenditure?"
    The price rise is bigger than the consumption cut, so expenditure
    must RISE. Eliminate every "decrease" option before computing.
```
